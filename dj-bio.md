# DJ Bio — Marco Valencia

Reference for Instagram and promo use.

**Vibe:** mysterious, dark, cool, sexy — minimal and refined, never overworked.

## Key facts

- Seattle underground. Current focus: progressive house (genre-fluid on purpose; always chasing the next sound before it becomes an official Beatport genre).
- Brought downtempo, playa tech, melodic techno and indie dance to Seattle.
- Came up promoting Studio 4/4 during the heyday of Thursdays at Q.
- Former resident DJ at The Underground.
- Opened for John Digweed, Enamour, Oliver Smith, Simon Vuarambon, Elif.
- Currently at the Monkey Loft.
